Agentic AI Development FAQs

What is agentic AI?

Agentic AI refers to systems that can plan, decide, and act across several steps to reach a goal, using tools and data rather than only answering a prompt. Human oversight sets the limits.

What is the difference between AI agents and agentic AI?

An AI agent is a single software component that completes tasks using tools. Agentic AI is the broader approach, where one or more agents work with planning, memory, and coordination to handle complex workflows.

Can AI agents automate a workflow end-to-end?

Yes, for many routine workflows. For high-risk steps we add approval points so a person reviews or overrides before the agent continues.

What type of agentic AI system should I choose?

It depends on the task. A single agent suits focused work, while multi-agent systems fit workflows that span several teams or systems. We recommend one after reviewing your use case, data, and risk level.

How can AI agents be customized to meet specific business needs?

We tailor agents to your processes, data, systems, and rules, including which actions they can take and when they must ask a person.

How much does AI agent development cost?

Cost depends on scope, number of integrations, data readiness, and the level of oversight needed. Book a consultation and we will outline a phased plan and estimate.

How do you ensure the security and privacy of our data?

We apply role-based access, encryption, and least-privilege access to systems, and we log every agent action. Estuate is ISO 27001 certified.
